# Master's Thesis: Vanishing Properties of Harmonic Functions

## Quick overview

An individual master's thesis in complex analysis investigating how zeros of complex-valued harmonic functions propagate along geometric curves in planar domains.

## What the work covers

- Harmonic and analytic functions in planar domains
- Uniqueness and vanishing conditions
- Zero sets with limit points
- Lines, circles, ellipses, parabolas, and hyperbolas
- Power-series expansions and analytic continuation
- Topological preliminaries involving connected components

## Main contributions

The thesis develops vanishing results first on connected components of the real axis and then extends them to several classes of planar curves. It also distinguishes local harmonic functions from the stronger conclusions available in the entire case.
