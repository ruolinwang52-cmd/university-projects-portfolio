# MATLAB dependencies

The submitted MATLAB scripts and their project-specific helper functions are included. Assignment 2 additionally loads `population_2024.mat`, a course-supplied dataset that is not redistributed in this public portfolio.

Toolbox functions used by the assignments include probability-distribution and sampling routines such as `mvnpdf`, `mvnrnd`, `randsample`, `gamrnd`, and Weibull distribution functions.
