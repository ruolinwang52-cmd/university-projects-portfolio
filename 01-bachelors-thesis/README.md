# Bachelor's Thesis: Vanishing of Harmonic Functions

## Quick overview

An individual pure-mathematics thesis on conditions under which a harmonic function that vanishes along local crossings of lines must vanish throughout a planar domain.

## What the work covers

- Harmonic and analytic functions on planar domains
- Uniqueness and vanishing results
- Power-series representations and admissible sets of directions
- Proof-based mathematical writing
