# Mathematical Modelling

## Quick overview

Two collaborative projects that translate open-ended business and biological problems into optimisation and dynamical-system models, then analyse them numerically and symbolically in Python.

## Assignment 1: consultancy cost optimisation

Models staffing and allocation decisions for a consultancy serving customers in several Swedish cities. The report formulates the constraints and objective, evaluates feasible staffing strategies, and interprets the resulting cost trade-offs.

## Assignment 2: rabbit–fox population dynamics

Builds and analyses a nonlinear predator-prey system with logistic prey growth. The work derives equilibria, studies stability through Jacobian eigenvalues, explores carrying-capacity regimes, and visualises phase-plane behaviour.

## Files

- `assignment-1-report.pdf`, `assignment-2-report.pdf` — complete reports
- `assignment-2.py` — numerical equilibrium, stability, and phase-plane analysis
- `project-1.ipynb`, `project-2.ipynb` — exploratory and symbolic calculations

## Skills demonstrated

Mathematical formulation, constrained optimisation, nonlinear ODEs, equilibrium and stability analysis, NumPy/SymPy workflows, visualisation, and interpretation of model assumptions.
