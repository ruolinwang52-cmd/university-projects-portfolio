# Derivative Pricing

## Quick overview

MATLAB implementations of discrete- and continuous-time derivative-pricing methods, with numerical comparisons against analytical benchmarks.

## Topics covered

- European and American option pricing with binomial trees
- Risk-neutral valuation and backward induction
- Black-Scholes pricing
- Monte Carlo pricing under geometric Brownian motion
- Antithetic variables and control variates
- Simulation error and convergence analysis

## Files

- `lab1.m` — binomial-tree and discrete-time pricing exercises
- `lab2.m` — Monte Carlo pricing, variance reduction, and continuous-time simulations
- [`DEPENDENCIES.md`](DEPENDENCIES.md) — self-contained and optional external function audit

## Skills demonstrated

Quantitative finance, numerical simulation, algorithm implementation, convergence assessment, and validation against closed-form results.
